# FarScore - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025-10-18_

FarScore is developed and published by **Jøgl & Pangel ApS** (hereafter referred to as “the developer”).

## Data storage and use

All data you enter into the app is stored locally on your device.

This data may include player names, scores, and game details.
Such information is processed solely for the purpose of enabling and improving the app’s core functionality.

None of this data is ever transmitted to the developer or any third parties.

If you have **iCloud Backup** enabled on your iPhone or iPad, your app data may be included in your personal backups. This process is managed entirely by Apple, and the developer has no access to or control over your iCloud data.

## Data collection and sharing

* FarScore does **not** send any data to the developer, Apple, or any third parties.
* The app uses **no analytics, advertising, or tracking** of any kind.
* The app makes **no network requests** and does **not** collect any identifiers (such as your IP address or device ID).

## Your control over your data

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, you retain full control of it.

* **Access and correction:** You can view and modify all of your data directly within the app at any time.
* **Deletion:** You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app.
* **Backups:** If you wish to remove any copies of your app data included in your iCloud Backup, you can do so manually from your device’s iCloud settings.
